Frequently asked questions

What licenses and insurance should a landscape contractor carry in Pensacola?

While Florida does not issue a general statewide landscaping license, contractors must maintain commercial general liability and workers' compensation insurance. Application of commercial fertilizers and pesticides legally requires Florida Department of Agriculture and Consumer Services (FDACS) certification, and backflow testing requires licensed cross-connection technicians.

How frequently should residential properties receive landscaping maintenance in Pensacola?

During the peak growing season from April through October, weekly turf mowing, edge trimming, and irrigation checks are standard. During slower winter months, service transitions to bi-weekly visits focusing on bed weed suppression, structural pruning, and debris removal.
