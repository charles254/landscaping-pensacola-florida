Bed Geometry, Steel Edging & Moisture-Retaining Mulch Layering

Clean bed edges and proper mulching protect plant roots from extreme summer soil temperatures while suppressing weed invasion. Designers utilize continuous steel or masonry edging to define turf boundaries and apply clean pine straw or shredded bark at optimal 3-inch depths.

Frequently asked questions

What flowering shrubs perform most reliably in Pensacola gardens?

Encore azaleas, loropetalum (Chinese fringe flower), camellias, and hydrangeas thrive in Pensacola conditions when provided with afternoon shade and well-drained, acidic sandy soil amended with organic compost.

What is the optimal mulch depth for landscape beds in Northwest Florida?

A uniform depth of 2 to 3 inches is recommended. Applying mulch deeper than 3 inches can suffocate root systems and cause moisture rot around plant stems, while less than 2 inches fails to block weed germination.
